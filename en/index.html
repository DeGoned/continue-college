About Us

SZTU Continuing Education College is a vital component of the university's talent development system, leveraging its strong engineering background and industry-education integration to serve the Greater Bay Area.

We offer high-level degree-based continuing education and non-degree training programs, serving as a key base for cultivating applied senior technical talent in Shenzhen and the Greater Bay Area.
